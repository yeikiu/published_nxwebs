Joshua Van arrives as champion with a clear advantage to retain the flyweight title against the top contender.

Alexandre Pantoja, #1 in the flyweight rankings, will look to reverse the forecast backed by historic 95/100 control.

The fight could go the distance, with decision as the most likely outcome.

Joshua Van defends the flyweight belt with a total career record of 17-2-0, and over the last 3 years shows a dominant 9-1-0 in 10 fights, a single loss that hasn't slowed his rise. His radar profile shows a takedown offense of 73 and a takedown defense of 75, figures that suggest near-total control of wherever he decides to take the fight, plus balanced striking (58 offense, 57 defense) that lets him sustain exchanges without overexposing himself.

On the other side, Alexandre Pantoja, #1 in the flyweight rankings, has accumulated a total record of 30-6-0 and over the last 36 months shows 4-1-0 in 5 fights, with a recent loss that weighs on the radar's reading. His great strength lies in control, with an outstanding score of 95/100, and a takedown defense of 89 that make him a serious obstacle for anyone attempting to submit him on the ground. However, his finishing threat (50) falls below the offensive combination the champion presents, and that statistical difference tips the prediction. The fight is set for 5 rounds, margin enough for both to unleash their attrition game before it reaches the judges' scorecards.
